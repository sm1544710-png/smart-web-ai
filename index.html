Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you decide.

Is the first website plan free?

Yes. You can request an initial plan without paying. Any build, domain, hosting or paid service will be discussed and approved separately.

Do you build websites for every type of business?

We can assess many types of small businesses and confirm what is practical for your requirements.

Will my website be published automatically?

No. You review and approve the draft first. Publishing requires your approval and an agreed hosting setup.

Can I edit the website later?

That depends on the platform and package selected. We will explain the editing options before work begins.
